1. About these terms

By booking or paying for a service, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old, that you have the authority to arrange services at the service address, and that you agree to these Terms. We may update these Terms from time to time; the version in effect on the date of your booking applies to that booking.

2. Bookings

Bookings are confirmed by email or text once scheduled. Arrival times are given as a window, and we will let you know if we are running late. Prices and service times are based on the details you provide, so please describe your home accurately — including its size, condition and any extra tasks.

To help us do our best work, please:

  • Give us safe access to your home at the agreed time, including keys, door codes or alarm instructions if you won't be home.
  • Tell us about pets, alarms, fragile or valuable items and any surfaces that need special care.
  • Make sure running water, electricity and adequate lighting are available.
  • Put away cash, jewelry and other valuables, and secure pets that may be anxious around visitors or equipment.

If the condition of your home is very different from what was described, we will talk with you before starting. We may adjust the price or time, or reschedule. Our team may decline any task that is unsafe, such as work at unsafe heights or areas with biohazards, pests or mold.

3. Rescheduling and cancellations

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your visit by calling, texting or emailing us.

  • Late changes: cancellations or reschedules made less than 48 hours before the visit may be charged a late cancellation fee of up to 50% of the visit price.
  • Lockouts: if our team arrives and cannot access your home at the agreed time, the visit may be charged at the full price.
  • Our changes: occasionally we may need to reschedule because of illness, severe weather or other events outside our control. We will contact you as early as possible and offer the next available time at no extra cost.

4. Payments

Prices are shown before you pay. Payments are processed securely online through our payment provider, and we accept major credit and debit cards.

  • Single visits are paid at the time of booking.
  • Packages and bundles are paid in full at purchase and are valid for 12 months from the purchase date. Unused package visits are not refundable once the package expires.
  • Add-ons requested on the day are charged at the listed price once completed.

If a payment fails, we may pause upcoming visits until it is resolved. Refunds, when approved, are returned to the original payment method.

5. Memberships

Memberships renew automatically each month on your billing date until you cancel. Your membership price, number of visits and member discounts are shown on our Membership page and in your confirmation.

  • You can pause or cancel online, or by contacting us, any time before your next billing date. There are no cancellation fees.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and visits already paid for can still be used during that period.
  • Included visits do not roll over to the next month unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • We will give at least 30 days' notice before any change to your membership price.

6. Our happiness promise

If something is not right, tell us within 24 hours of your visit and we will come back to re-clean the areas in question at no cost. Photos help us understand the issue and fix it quickly.

The happiness promise covers tasks included in the service you booked. It does not cover areas we could not access, tasks outside the booked service, or conditions that existed before the visit, such as permanent stains, wear or damage.

7. Damage and liability

We are bonded and insured, and our team takes great care in your home. Accidents are rare, but if something is damaged during a visit, please report it within 48 hours with photos so we can make it right. Depending on the situation, we will repair the item, replace it or reimburse its reasonable value.

We are not responsible for:

  • Damage caused by pre-existing wear, faulty installation or items that were already loose, cracked or unstable.
  • Stains, discoloration or fabric reactions that cannot be removed or that result from the age or condition of the material, after we have inspected and pre-tested it.
  • Items of high or sentimental value, such as cash, jewelry or heirlooms, that were not put away or pointed out to us before the visit.
  • Delays or missed visits caused by events outside our reasonable control.

To the extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim related to a service is limited to the amount you paid for that service, except where damage is covered by our insurance. These Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.
