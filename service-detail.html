The 98%-dry promise

No soggy carpets. No damp curtains.

Most steam jobs leave things wet for a day or more. Our dry-out step means your carpets, curtains and windows are about 98% dry before we leave, so you can use your rooms the same day.

If anything still feels damp the next day, give us a call within 24 hours and we will come back and dry it out, free of charge.
