Steam cleaning

How often should you steam-clean your curtains?

Bees Home Cleaning team November 2026 · 4 min read
Share

Curtains are one of the most forgotten things in a home. They hang quietly for months, catching dust, pollen and cooking smells every single day. Because they're fabric, they hold on to all of it — until you open them on a sunny morning and see the dust float out.

Why curtains get dirty so fast

Curtains sit right next to windows, where fresh air, pollen and outdoor dust come in. They also catch everything that rises from the room: cooking oils, pet hair and household dust. Heavy fabrics and lined curtains trap even more, deep in the weave where a quick shake can't reach.

A simple room-by-room schedule

  • Kitchen: every 3 months. Cooking oils and smells cling to fabric quickly.
  • Living room: every 6 months. High traffic, sunlight and dust from everyday life.
  • Bedrooms: every 6 months, or every 3 months if anyone has allergies.
  • Kids' rooms and nurseries: every 3–4 months to keep dust and allergens down.
  • Guest and spare rooms: once a year, or before visitors arrive.

Why steam is best for curtains

Many curtains can't go in the washing machine — they shrink, lose their shape or have linings that pull. Steam cleans them in place. Hot steam loosens dust and kills dust mites without soaking the fabric, and there's no need to take them down, wash them and rehang them.

“The heavier the curtain, the more it holds. A good steam clean is often the difference between a stuffy bedroom and a fresh one.”

— Bees Home Cleaning team

Keep them fresh in between

  • Once a month, run the vacuum brush attachment over curtains from top to bottom.
  • Open curtains and windows for ten minutes a day to let fresh air move through.
  • Keep pets from napping on low-hanging fabric where possible.
  • Spot-blot marks with a damp white cloth — never rub.

Signs your curtains need cleaning now

Visible dust when you draw them, a stale smell when the room warms up, faded or greyish edges, or sneezing in a particular room are all clear signs it's time. If you can't remember the last time they were cleaned, that's a sign too.
