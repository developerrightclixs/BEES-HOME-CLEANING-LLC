Our story

Started in Findlay. Built on trust.

Bees Home Cleaning started with a simple frustration: too many cleaners rushed the job, and carpets and curtains were left soaking wet for days.

So we did it differently. We invested in professional steam equipment and a dry-out process, so your carpets, curtains and bathroom windows are steam-cleaned and dried before we leave — about 98% dry.

Today we serve families across Findlay and nearby towns. Many of them have been with us for years. They know our team by name, and we know how they like their homes.
