Membership savings

How a cleaning membership saves you money every year

Bees Home Cleaning team November 2026 · 4 min read
Share

Booking a cleaner only when the house gets out of hand feels like the cheaper option. In practice, it usually costs more. A regular membership keeps your home at the "easy" stage, gives you a team that knows your house, and takes money off every extra you add.

Catch-up cleans cost more than you think

When weeks go by between cleans, dust mixes with kitchen grease, soap scum hardens and grime settles into grout and carpets. Removing that takes far more time and effort than a routine visit — and time is what you pay for. Regular visits stop that build-up before it starts, so each clean stays quick and affordable.

Members save on every extra

Every Bees membership includes a discount on add-ons and packages, so the deeper jobs cost less when you need them.

  • Bee Basic — $100 / month: one standard clean a month and 10% off add-ons.
  • Busy Bee — $120 / month: two standard cleans a month, a steam-clean of one room per quarter and 15% off add-ons & packages.
  • Queen Bee — $140 / month: four standard cleans a month, a steam-clean of one room each month and 20% off add-ons & packages.

The same trusted team

Members get the same crew on every visit. They learn where things go, which rooms matter most to you and how your pets like to be handled. Less explaining and less time spent getting oriented means every visit goes further.

“Our members' homes are the easiest to clean — because they never get the chance to get dirty.”

— Bees Home Cleaning team

Protect what you've already paid for

Carpets, grout, curtains and windows last longer when they're cleaned on a schedule. Grit wears down carpet fibers, and hard-water spots etch glass over time. Regular care means fewer replacements and fewer expensive restoration jobs down the road.

No long contracts

Memberships are pay-online and cancel any time. If a visit isn't right, we offer a free re-clean within 24 hours. You keep the savings without being locked in.

Is a membership right for you?

If you'd book at least one clean a month anyway — or you keep putting off a big clean because of the cost — a membership almost always works out cheaper. Pick the plan that matches how busy your home is, and let the savings add up visit after visit.
