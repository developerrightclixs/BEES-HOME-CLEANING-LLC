The short version: we collect only what we need to book, clean and bill your home. We never sell your information, and you can ask us to update or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Bees Home Cleaning LLC (“Bees Home Cleaning”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides residential cleaning, steam cleaning and related services in and around Sidney, Ohio 45840. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, book a service, buy a package or membership, apply for a job or contact us — and how we use and protect it.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or share your information with us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you book, apply for a job or contact us, we collect details such as:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and service address.
  • Notes about your home, such as its size, the rooms or items to be cleaned, pets, alarm or entry instructions, and any special requests.
  • Your preferred contact method and the messages you send us.
  • For job applicants: your work history, availability and any other details you include in your application.

Payment information

When you pay online, your payment details are entered into and processed by our secure third-party payment processor. We receive confirmation of the payment and limited details such as the card type and last four digits. We do not see or store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, device type, pages viewed, the website that referred you and the date and time of your visit. This information does not directly identify you.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule and carry out cleanings, and send booking confirmations and appointment reminders by email or text.
  • Take payments, manage packages and memberships, and keep accurate business and tax records.
  • Answer your questions, follow up on feedback and honor our happiness promise.
  • Review job applications and contact applicants.
  • Improve our services, website and booking experience.
  • Protect the safety and security of our customers, team and business, and meet our legal obligations.

We only send marketing emails, such as cleaning tips and member deals, if you sign up for them. Every marketing email includes a link to unsubscribe.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to run our business, with:

  • Our cleaning team — the team members assigned to your visit see the details they need to do the job, such as your address and home notes.
  • Service providers who help us operate, such as payment, scheduling, email and website hosting tools. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons — when required by law, court order or government request, or when necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, team or others.
  • Business changes — if our business is sold or merged, customer information may be transferred to the new owner, who must continue to protect it in line with this policy.

5. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Essential cookies make features like online booking and payment work. Analytics cookies help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you block essential cookies, some parts of the website, such as online booking, may not work properly.

6. Keeping your information safe

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, and we limit access to the people who need it to serve you. Entry codes and key details are shared only with the team members assigned to your home.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, or as required for legal, tax and accounting reasons. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we take protecting your information seriously.

7. Your choices

You are in control of your information. You can:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update your details.
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we need to keep it to complete a service, meet a legal obligation or keep required records.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us.
  • Opt out of text reminders by replying STOP or by letting us know.

We will respond to your request within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website or shared by email.
